# Experimental protocol

## Source and scope

The six-page project specification defines O1–O12 and proposed targets on pp. 1–3. The local source directory contains 75 HEIC photos. Metadata places all photos within 18 minutes and 4 seconds on November 7, 2026, on the same iPhone 17 Pro Max ultrawide camera. All raw rasters are 4032 × 3024. The source includes a byte-identical copy of IMG_3215. Contact sheets cover every frame. One syringe appears per frame. A hand appears in most photos, and repeated framing, physical appearance, and contiguous timestamps suggest object reuse. Distinct physical-unit identity cannot be verified from pixels alone. There are no known negative scenes, simultaneous instances, validated temporal sequences, or reference inventories.

Visible conditions include dark surfaces, pale surfaces, a wooden surface, a window or reflective surround, directional shadows, glare, rotation, distance changes, hand occlusion and image-edge truncation. Grouping by timestamp alone would leak likely object identities. The development-only acquisition group includes the entire collection, even across the ten-minute pause. Sequence or frame resampling cannot estimate independent acquisition performance.

## Annotation

Label the complete visible syringe extent (no hands, shadows or unattached objects), including a visible attached tip, barrel, flanges and plunger. Use tight axis-aligned normalized xyxy boxes relative to the orientation-corrected image and clip at the image boundary. Do not extrapolate hidden parts. Barrel-axis landmarks and subregion boxes are a future annotation extension. Separate visible color from the color of the plastic, and from any inference about contents.

Type means visual scale family, either '20-scale' or '60-scale' for the two distinguishable printed scale patterns. These are visual labels. Neither model output nor an isolated OCR number establishes nominal capacity. Repeated burst views can support a draft identification of a visually matching instrument across images. Leave unverifiable cases unknown. Appearance color labels use 'amber-brown' and 'orange' for the colored region visible through the barrel. Lighting can move apparent shades between these categories. These annotations start as draft and require independent human approval before use as validated ground truth. Editorial provenance is recorded with the annotations and corrections are append-only events.

## Partitions and selection rules

- **Training/development** uses the 74 unique supplied images as one group. No supplied image is placed in validation or test.
- **External validation** uses a curated and visually annotated Wikimedia Commons sample of photographic objects with per-file license and attribution. It includes syringes across different views, one scene with two syringes, and optically similar negatives. The 19 selected records and their draft boxes are fixed by their annotated snapshot. Open Images V7 links to validation box and verified image-label files with zero syringe records, which made its validation partition unsuitable.
- **External test** uses the official Open Images test partition. Include individually boxed Syringe scenes and explicit verified negatives. Exclude images containing a group-of, depiction or inside box for the category. Require recoverable CC BY 2.0 attribution metadata and keep at most 40 verified negatives in sorted ID order. Reserve test pixels until checkpoint, prompt and operating threshold are frozen. The independent test measures localization on an external photographic sample with distinct annotation conventions and prevalence. Pretraining overlap cannot be ruled out, and the sample gives no direct evidence for generalization to intended acquisition conditions.
- Compare source URLs, file hashes, 64-bit difference hashes at Hamming distance ≤4, acquisition groups and available normalized author names across partitions. Reject colliding test images before inference. Author aliases and near-duplicates with large crops or viewpoint change can escape these checks. Keep all boxes in an eligible image together.
- Compare Grounding DINO Tiny and OWLv2 Base at frozen prompt and compute settings, plus a locally trained proposal re-ranker with and without geometric/photometric augmentation. Keep candidate scores down to 0.02 for raw inference and use IoU 0.6 NMS for all variants. Fit the proposal learner only on the supplied group. Rank candidates by validation mean AP over IoU .50:.05:.95, with AP50 as the tiebreaker. Select the operational score threshold on validation by F1 at IoU .50, breaking ties in favor of precision and then the higher threshold. Save this selection before preparing test pixels. Apply only the frozen winner to the independent test.
- Report precision, recall, F1, AP, exact count, false positives and negatives, observable robustness slices and latency. Scene aggregates and slices include denominators. Open Images has no equivalent scale or color labels. Attribute experiments are development diagnostics and remain separately identified. Report the coverage of abstention and distinguish score from calibrated probability.
- Runtime stages pass when inputs and artifact hashes match, annotations and exported formats validate, key logic has automated tests, the selected test run is tied to the freeze record, and the local UI/API give inspectable real predictions and auditable corrections. Numerical targets are outcomes to evaluate.

## Priority and feasibility

Run the two CPU inference alternatives locally and train a small proposal learner atop the frozen features. Full detector fine-tuning on repeated single-object frames risks teaching acquisition-specific shortcuts. It cannot solve the lack of independent local acquisitions. Intel Core Ultra 7 258V, eight logical cores, 30 GiB RAM and 117 GiB free at audit time. Intel Arc shared-memory graphics is visible via Vulkan and Level Zero, with no CUDA device identified. CPU is the reproducibility target. Remote compute is unnecessary for the planned experiments. Type/color validation, unseen-type rejection, time savings and physical tracking need specifically collected reference evidence.
