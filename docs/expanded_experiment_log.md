# Expanded collection experiment log

## 2026-11-20 — Intake and evaluation design

The expanded delivery contains 177 JPEGs, IMG_3161 through IMG_3337. All have capture date November 7, 2026, on the same iPhone 17 Pro Max. There are 97 files at 5712 × 4284 and 80 at 4032 × 3024 before orientation. All have a Display P3 profile. The old inventory contributes 74 matching frame IDs. The largest luminance dHash distance for a same-ID JPEG/HEIC pair is three of 64 bits. The remaining 103 frame IDs are new to this project. Treat matched exports as the same observations regardless of their file hashes.

The full chronological contact sheets show one visible syringe in each frame, two recognizable graduation layouts, uncolored barrels, dark amber and orange interiors, varied apparent lightness, different rotations, close-ups, clipped objects, shadows, distinct backgrounds and specular highlights. The images belong to a single collection session. The same physical syringe may recur after its contents or surroundings change. All 177 scenes are positive, so the collection alone cannot establish specificity against pens, pipettes or empty scenes. No separate optical reference for opacity was found during intake.

Reserve the new clear 20-scale acquisition block (IMG_3178–IMG_3191) for an untouched expanded-domain control. The adjacent clear 60-scale acquisition block remains available for development. Keep confirmed visually similar neighbors and repeated acquisition sequences together. Use disjoint reviewed blocks within the filled captures to calibrate, and preserve the existing public reserve as regression evidence rather than reuse it to fit the new hybrid. Record separately the results that may inherit earlier exposure to HEIC versions. An independent second capture or physical-specimen ground truth remains unavailable.

The first detection experiments compare the deployed automatic cascade, component-only predictors, additive fusion, agreement-gated fusion, and a trainable proposal ranker. Compare matched instances individually in addition to precision, recall, F1, exact scenes and AP. Keep rescue thresholds internal. Use metadata only for grouping and provenance. Keep OCR legibility, capacity, scale, localized color and apparent lightness as separate tasks. Reuse SAM 3 and frozen DINO features from the verified local environment first. Meta's official SAM 3 materials document text and exemplar prompts for multiple matching objects. DINOv3 is a potential dense-backbone comparator if corpus size, local resources and access support a controlled comparison. [SAM 3 model card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/sam3) · [DINOv3 research](https://ai.meta.com/research/dinov3/)

## First model selection and subsequent error checks

- Detector search evaluated 492 configurations, including each detector alone, simple union, cross-source score agreement, logistic reranking, histogram gradient boosting, a burst-balanced objective, 0.50/0.70 localization targets, three box policies, and additive retention. The first calibration winner reduced aggregate false positives from 23 to 7 with 68 matches in both versions. A fit replay exposed two lost public matches. A stricter selection used those fit cases and calibration only. Twelve settings met the no-lost-matches rule on both partitions and improved calibration F1. The new selection uses all three branches, a logistic head trained at IoU 0.70 and mask-supported boxes. The initial and stricter selections both remain in the archive. [Search](../artifacts/expanded/detector_trials.json) · [Strict study](../artifacts/expanded/detector_recall_freeze.json)
- Four apparent localization errors in fit or calibration pointed to imprecise draft boxes. An enlarged review corrected their visible bounds in a separate manifest. The frozen comparisons retain the original labels. [Post-score adjudication](../artifacts/expanded/annotations_adjudicated.json)
- OCR tested the original reader, CLAHE, sharpening with percentile contrast, and agreement between both alternate views. On 68 calibration crops the counts of correct capacity readings were 38, 51, 44 and 44 respectively, with zero observed wrong outputs. The conservative agreement system was frozen. Its held-out raw reader preserved the original eight correct capacities and inherited a mistaken 26 mL reading. A follow-up corroboration rule for any untyped capacity removed that reading and retained all correct answers on replay. The detected defect remains documented as a test-guided regression, and separate 60-scale rotation controls are provided. [OCR calibration](../artifacts/expanded/markings_calibration.json) · [Held-out raw readings](../artifacts/expanded/markings_test.json) · [Corroboration replay](../artifacts/expanded/markings_guard.json)
- The appearance study compared color-only segmentation, the current DINOv2 head, and 24 new trained head and cutoff settings. The frozen model with ICC-aware Lab output had 59 supported colored measurements on 68 calibration views compared with 58 for the existing head and 51 for color alone. Both learned models emitted one false color on the 14 clear views. A second study froze a surrounding-color comparison on calibration data and removed that error on replay. Of six newly reviewed public appearance controls, two still had false liquid color, involving a colored backdrop and a syringe label. The candidate failed its release criterion. [Regional calibration](../artifacts/expanded/liquid_calibration.json) · [Clear holdout](../artifacts/expanded/liquid_test.json) · [Public color controls](../artifacts/expanded/color_context_audit.json)
- Eight clear-vs-colored photographs with superficially similar poses were compared for optical reference suitability. All eight pairs had exposure differences and shared no controlled texture correspondence. The dataset still does not support calibrated opacity. [Pair check](../artifacts/expanded/opacity_audit.json)

## Apparent fill follow-up

Tested 36 combinations of OCR confidence, contrast at the piston edge, continuity of colored contents and a fallback based on periodic graduation marks.
